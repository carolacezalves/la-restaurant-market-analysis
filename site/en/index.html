Network thesis

Chains comprise 38.1% of classified venues, showing that multi-location operations are established in this market. It does not show that this concept can scale profitably.

Expand when one unit demonstrates reliable service and attractive economics through ordinary weeks, across customer groups and service modes.

Decision today: fund a measured pilot with three capacity scenarios and meaningful customer choice. Select a footprint only after observed behavior and site economics support it.

Limitations: no sales, rents, traffic, customer preferences, opening dates, robot cost or reliability measures. The inventory is descriptive context, not a forecast.
